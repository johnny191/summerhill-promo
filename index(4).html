DEPOT INFORMATION DISPLAY

Promotional Video — Visual Asset Library & Creative Brief

PROJECT PURPOSE

This page is the visual reference library for creating a professional promotional video for the Depot Information Display.

The system uses existing depot TVs and screens to provide live, useful information to staff. Head Office can update information remotely through a browser, allowing important information to reach individual depots quickly without relying entirely on printed notices.

IMPORTANT — USE THE SUPPLIED PRODUCT IMAGES AS AUTHORITATIVE.
The product screenshots in this directory show the real system. Do not invent, redesign or recreate the user interface, logos, QR codes or written content. AI-generated video should be used for environments, people, cameras and cinematic scenes; supplied screenshots should be used whenever the actual product UI is shown.

THE CREATIVE STORY

1. THE OLD WAY — PROBLEM

Begin sombre and initially in black and white. Show real depot environments, switched-off TVs and noticeboards covered with printed paper. The feeling should be slightly dull and frustrating rather than exaggerated.

2. THE REALISATION

Someone notices an unused screen and the surrounding paper notices. There is a simple moment of realisation: “Why don't we use the screens?”

3. THE EUREKA MOMENT

The screen is switched on. The visual world transitions from black and white into full colour and the music becomes modern, energetic and positive.

4. THE SOLUTION

Reveal the real Depot Information Display and demonstrate what it can do.

5. THE PAYOFF

Finish with active screens, live information and the idea that the organisation already had the hardware — it simply needed to make the screens work for everyone.

KEY PRODUCT FEATURES TO SHOW

  • Real-time information displayed on existing depot TVs.
  • Remote updates from Head Office through a browser.
  • Real-time messages from Management.
  • Important information from Central Control.
  • Live news ticker.
  • Live weather information.
  • Localised information for individual depots, such as Summerhill.
  • Multiple information pages, with page counters such as “1 OF 7”, “2 OF 7” and “4 OF 7”.
  • Information such as overtime, health and wellbeing and depot notices.
  • QR codes where they appear in the supplied genuine screenshots.
  • System operating across multiple locations — currently 10 locations.
  • Reduced reliance on printed notices and associated paper waste. Do not claim the system is completely paperless.

Asset handling rule: If an image is a genuine product screenshot, preserve its UI, text, logos, page numbers and QR codes. Use AI generation for cinematic environments and people, not for recreating the product interface.

The assets may include:

VIDEO DIRECTION

Recommended format: 16:9 landscape, approximately 75–90 seconds.

Emotional progression: FRUSTRATION → REALISATION → EUREKA → EXCITEMENT → DEMONSTRATION → BENEFITS → SCALE → PAYOFF

Opening: sombre, black and white, subdued piano, switched-off screens and paper notices.

Transition: moment of silence at the “Why don't we use the screens?” realisation, followed by colour and energetic modern music.

Middle: fast, polished demonstrations of the genuine product screenshots and key features.

Ending: busy depot, working screen, strong visual payoff.

Suggested closing line:

“The screens were already there. Now they're working for us.”

AI / RUNWAY RULES

  • Do not generate fake versions of the Depot Information Display interface.
  • Do not invent text, page numbers, QR codes or logos.
  • Do not distort or replace supplied product screenshots.
  • Use AI generation primarily for people, depot environments, cinematic camera movement and atmosphere.
  • Use supplied real photographs for the “before” story whenever possible.
  • Keep the depot environment believable and grounded in a real Irish public-transport workplace.
  • Avoid futuristic holograms, sci-fi interfaces and generic technology advertising clichés.
  • The finished video should feel like a professional internal/corporate technology launch, not a generic AI advert.

RUNWAY / PRODUCTION PLAN

First inspect all files in this directory and README.txt. Identify the actual filename of every useful image.

Then create a complete production plan including:

  • Overall creative concept
  • Full storyboard
  • Exact shot-by-shot timeline
  • Voiceover/narration
  • On-screen text
  • Music and sound effects
  • Camera and lighting direction
  • Which supplied asset is used in every shot
  • Which shots should be generated in Runway
  • Which shots should use real photographs/screenshots
  • Detailed Runway prompt for every AI-generated shot
  • Instructions for compositing genuine screenshots onto generated TV screens
  • Final 75–90 second editing sequence

Do not jump straight into generating random clips. First understand the supplied material and build the story around it.
